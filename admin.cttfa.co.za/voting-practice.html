Accreditation — at the door

Check delegates in. A full club gets 2 votes; if a second delegate from the same full club checks in, the two votes split 1 + 1 and each gets their own code. An associate club has one delegate, one vote — a second is refused.

Cast the votes

One simulated phone per accredited delegate. Tap a choice and Confirm — the club's weight goes one way, as one sealed ballot. No running tally is shown while the motion is open.
