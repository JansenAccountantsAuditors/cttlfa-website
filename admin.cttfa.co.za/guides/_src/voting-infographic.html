How CTTLFA voting works — who votes, and what it takes

vote.cttfa.co.za · a weighted, in-person ballot grounded in the 2026 Constitution

CTTLFA decisions are taken by a weighted vote of the member clubs, in person at a general meeting. How much a club counts, whether it may vote at all, and what it takes for a motion to pass are all set by the Constitution. The Admin Centre runs that ballot electronically, exactly to those rules.

Before a club may vote

Four eligibility gates, checked at the door — Articles 6 & 8

In good standing

Fees paid and the club compliant for the meeting. A non-compliant club is not accredited and is left out of the voting strength.

Present in person

Only clubs present vote. The delegate is named 24 hours prior; a substitute needs a written mandate.

Credentials confirmed

A valid SAFA registration card and the delegate's identity are confirmed at accreditation before a code is issued.

Meeting is quorate

Two limbs: more than half of Mancom and more than half of clubs (26 of 50). The chair confirms both before any motion opens.

How CTTLFA voting works — from the desk to the result

Accreditation to record, run cleanly and secretly from the room

Every motion runs through the same seven steps. A club is accredited once, registers one delegate, and receives a single code that casts one sealed ballot on each motion — counted at the club's full weight, measured against the rule, and written into the record.

From accreditation to result

Seven steps, the same for every motion on the agenda
1

Accreditation

The desk accredits each present, compliant club, which registers one delegate. That delegate carries the club's whole vote as a block: a full club 2 votes, an associate 1. A second delegate is not accredited.

2

Code issued

One single-use code per club, tied to its voting weight. Handed over as a printed slip or on-screen QR at check-in.

3

Or sent by SMS

The same code can be sent to the delegate's phone by SMS as the electronic fallback.

CTTLFA voting: your code is XXXXXX. Vote at vote.cttfa.co.za (one phone only).
4

Delegate opens the ballot

The delegate scans the QR or types the code at vote.cttfa.co.za. The code binds to that one phone — a second device is refused.

5

Chair opens the motion

The chair starts the motion with its pass rule and a countdown timer. It auto-closes at zero, or the chair closes it early. No votes after close.

6

Cast — Yes / No / Abstain

The delegate confirms one choice. The club's full weight goes one way (block vote), one sealed ballot per club per motion. No live tally is shown while open.

7

Weighted tally & result

Votes are counted at weight. Accredited clubs that did not vote count as abstentions. The result is measured on the motion's rule — Carried or Not carried — and recorded.

Built to be trusted

Six controls make the count secret, weighted and defensible

Sealed ballot

No one — not even an admin — sees a running tally while a motion is open. The count is revealed only on close.

One code, one phone

Each club's code binds to the first device and casts one ballot per motion. No double voting, no shared codes.

Weighted, block vote

A full club's two votes move together at full weight, an associate's one — exactly as Article 8.2 requires.

Implied abstentions

Voting strength is the accredited, eligible weight. A club that does not vote is counted as an abstention, shown as “no-show”.

Room view

A projector page shows the countdown while open and Carried / Not carried on close — never the live tally.

Audit & record

Who accredited, who opened and closed each motion is logged. Attendance and result PDFs go straight into the minute book.

GOOD TO KNOW

The system counts exactly to the Constitution — full clubs two, associates one, thresholds by motion type.

In person and in the room: codes handed over at check-in, sealed ballots, a result on the screen.

Data stays in-house on the Association's own system — delegates' details are not sent to an outside voting service.

Presented to the Management Committee (Mancom).
