Nominate for the 2026 awards

Every member club may nominate for any of the eight awards below. The Management Committee reviews all nominations and decides the winners, guided by your motivations.

Nominations are open

How it works
  • Choose an award, tell us who you are nominating, and write a short motivation. The motivation is what the panel weighs most — be specific and give examples.
  • You may nominate for as many awards as you like, and more than one nominee per award. Submit one at a time.
  • The nominee should be a club, player or coach registered with the Association and in good standing for 2026.
  • We email you a confirmation of each nomination, so please use an address you check.
  • Nominations are confidential to the Association and used only for the awards process.
Nominations are now closed. Please contact the CTTLFA office if you still need to submit one.
