C02: club obligations register — standing with the Association

Adds a "Your standing with the Association" register to the club portal
overview, below the stats. Shows finance, delegate voting, membership and
player registrations as distinct lines, each with its own status and the
authority that governs it. Deliberately no single compliance score: a
finance or voting standing is stated NOT to be a match-eligibility
decision, which is governed separately under the competition rules. Uses
existing portal data only (portal_home / portal_registrations); no new
backend.
